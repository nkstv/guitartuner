Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Guitar Tuner Fast Chrome extension. The extension is designed to work locally in your browser and to collect as little information as possible.

01 — Data collection

We do not collect personal data

Guitar Tuner Fast does not collect, sell or transmit personally identifiable information, browsing history, website content, financial information, authentication information, location data or personal communications.

02 — Microphone

Microphone audio stays on your device

Microphone access is optional and is used only when you activate microphone tuning. Audio is analyzed in real time inside your browser to estimate pitch and help you tune your instrument.

  • Microphone audio is not recorded.
  • Microphone audio is not stored.
  • Microphone audio is not uploaded or transmitted to our servers or to third parties.
  • Microphone access stops when the tuning session is stopped or the extension popup is closed.

03 — Local storage

Only your preferences are saved

The extension uses Chrome local extension storage to remember basic preferences such as your selected language, last selected instrument and tuning. This information stays in your browser and is used only to make the extension more convenient to use.

04 — Remote code & third parties

No remote code or advertising trackers

All JavaScript and application logic required by the extension are included in the extension package. The extension does not execute remotely hosted JavaScript or WebAssembly and does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

05 — Data sharing

We do not sell or transfer user data

Guitar Tuner Fast does not sell user data and does not transfer user data to third parties. Data is not used for advertising, creditworthiness, lending decisions or any purpose unrelated to the extension's core tuning functionality.

06 — Changes

Updates to this policy

If the extension's privacy practices change, this page will be updated before or alongside the relevant extension update.

Last updated: September 13, 2026.

Questions about privacy
